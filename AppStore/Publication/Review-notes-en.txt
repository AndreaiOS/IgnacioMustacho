Ignacio Mustacho is a single-player rhythm game for iPhone, in English. No ball or hoop is used: the player performs four shadow-basketball moves.

No app account or sign-in is required for offline gameplay. On Home, How to Play explains the gestures. Tutorial starts four slower commands without life penalties, followed by a normal run. Start a gesture when NOW appears and the dot is inside the outlined hit zone. The buttons under the timing bar offer the same four actions.

Scores are 1 for Good and 2 for Perfect, with combo multipliers up to x3. The tempo increases every four commands, reaching 320 BPM at command 129. Milestones at 10, 23, 33, 50, 81 and 100 points pause the game clock for a brief celebration. The ending joke is text only; spoken move calls can be disabled separately in Sound check. Sound respects Silent mode.

Game Center is optional. To access it, open Club records and select Connect Game Center. The app uses Apple's account interface, not a developer-operated account. Run scores are submitted when authenticated. Award progress is stored locally and retried when authenticated; local awards may sync on connection. The local game remains available offline. Leaderboard and achievement identifiers are listed in the accompanying Game Center setup package.

Support and Privacy policy links are available in Sound check from Home or Pause. There are no ads, in-app purchases or subscriptions.
