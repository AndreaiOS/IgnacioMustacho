How do I play?

Tap to dribble. Swipe down for Between the legs, left or right for Behind the back, and up to shoot. Start your gesture when the centre of the moving dot is inside the outlined HIT ZONE and NOW appears. A short swipe is enough. The four buttons below the timing bar perform the same moves.

Where should I start?

Open How to Play or Tutorial from Home. The four-move warm-up does not cost lives, then a normal run begins. A normal run has three lives. A wrong move or missed beat costs one life.

Why does the game get faster?

The tempo rises every four commands, from 100 up to 320 BPM. The highest pace is reached at command 129. Scores depend on timing and combo; they are not the speed counter.

I cannot hear the game

Check your iPhone volume, Silent mode and connected headphones. In Sound check, Game audio controls all game sound. Spoken move calls can be switched off separately while keeping the organ and effects. The final joke is intentionally written, not spoken.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Your personal best, award progress and audio preferences are saved on this iPhone. The local record is not automatically copied between devices.

Game Center and missing scores

Open Club records and choose Connect Game Center to use Apple’s online features. Availability depends on your Apple account, connectivity and service availability. The game remains playable if Game Center is unavailable.

Scores are submitted at the end of a run when authenticated. Failed leaderboard submissions are not queued for automatic retry; the local personal best is still saved. Award progress is queued locally and retries while the app is active and Game Center is authenticated. Previously earned local awards may sync when you connect.

What happens if I change Game Center accounts?

Local progress belongs to the device. It is not a separate save for each Game Center account, and local awards can be reported to another account used on the same device.

How do I pause?

Tap Pause during a run. Leaving the app pauses play. Celebrations also stop the game clock; Keep playing skips the celebration and starts a fresh countdown.

Removing local progress

There is no in-app reset button. Deleting the app removes its local app data; offloading it keeps that data. Device backups may retain or restore data. Deleting the app does not delete information already held by Game Center. Contact support if you need help understanding what is stored.

Compatibility

iPhone with iOS 17 or later. The interface is in English and plays in portrait orientation. The app has no in-app purchases or subscription features.
