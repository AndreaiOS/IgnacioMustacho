Privacy policy

This policy describes Ignacio Mustacho for iPhone and its support website. The developer and contact for personal information handled through support is Andrea Murru, an.murru@gmail.com.

Playing on your iPhone

The app does not operate a developer-run account system or game server. It contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, no tracking, and no in-app purchases. It does not request access to your contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone. Spoken move calls use speech output; the app does not record your voice.

The app stores your best score, audio preferences and award progress locally using iOS preferences. When Game Center is authenticated, it also stores your game-specific Game Center player identifier and synchronisation acknowledgements locally to avoid repeating successful award reports. This information is used to provide the game and keep track of progress.

Optional Apple Game Center

You can play offline without connecting Game Center. If you use Game Center, the app sends run scores and achievement progress to Apple through GameKit when authenticated. Pending award progress may be sent later when you connect. The developer does not receive these reports on a separate server.

Apple handles the Game Center account and service. Scores and profile information can be visible to other players according to Apple’s service and privacy settings. See Apple’s Game Center privacy information for details of Apple’s processing and controls.

Local progress belongs to the device rather than to a separate account save. Awards earned on that device may be synchronised to the Game Center account subsequently used on it. The app does not download and merge save data from other devices.

Support requests

If you email support, Andrea Murru receives your email address, message and any files or technical details you choose to include. These are used to respond to your request and investigate problems, not for advertising or sale. Email is processed by the email providers involved, including Google for the support mailbox; see Google’s privacy policy.

Support correspondence is retained only for as long as reasonably needed to handle the request, follow up on the issue or meet applicable legal obligations. Where applicable, responding to a support request is based on providing the requested assistance and the legitimate interest in maintaining and supporting the app.

This website

These pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising, embedded social media or developer-set cookies. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes technical requests, including visitor IP addresses for security. See GitHub’s privacy statement. The hosting and email providers may process information in countries other than yours, subject to their published privacy terms and safeguards.

Retention and your choices

Local game data remains until removed through iOS app-data controls. Deleting the app removes its local data; offloading the app does not. Copies may remain in backups controlled by your Apple settings. Removing local data does not erase scores or achievements already stored by Apple.

Manage Game Center account and sharing settings through Apple’s controls. For questions about support correspondence, or to request access, correction or deletion of personal information held by the developer, email an.murru@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to object to or restrict processing, obtain a copy of your information, or complain to your local data protection authority. Requests concerning Apple’s or GitHub’s own records should be directed to those providers.

Children

The app does not ask players to submit personal information or create a developer account. Apple’s account and parental controls apply to Game Center. A parent or guardian can contact support about personal information a child may have sent in a support request.
